Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize Sigma Publisher via TikTok Login Kit, we only receive the information and permissions you explicitly grant, which may include:

  • Basic TikTok profile identifiers (such as open_id, display name, and avatar URL).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to upload or publish videos on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

Collected data and tokens are used solely to authenticate your session and execute video upload or publishing actions requested by you through TikTok's official APIs.

3. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or TikTok data with third parties. Data is only transmitted to TikTok's official API endpoints as required to perform the requested actions.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

OAuth tokens are stored only as long as necessary to provide the Service. You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately invalidates active tokens.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored credentials and tokens against unauthorized access.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, contact the developer via GitHub at https://github.com/tony-ga.
