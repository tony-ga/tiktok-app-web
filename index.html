Σ Sigma Publisher

Sigma Publisher is a content creation and scheduling tool designed to help creators prepare, manage, and publish educational science and mathematics short-form videos to TikTok.

Using TikTok's official Content Posting API and Login Kit, authorized creators can securely connect their TikTok account and upload videos directly from their workflow.
